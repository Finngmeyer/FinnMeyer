Supply Chain Internship · Summer 2025

Bechtel — Hanford Waste Treatment Plant

Hanford Site · Richland, WA. Me on the left

The summer before my senior year I interned with Bechtel at the Hanford Waste Treatment Plant in Richland, WA. One of the most complex active construction projects in U.S. history, built to clean up nuclear waste left over from the Manhattan Project and Cold War era plutonium production.

As a supply chain intern on the Department of Energy cleanup project, I spent my days in the warehouse receiving, inspecting, and reconciling incoming materials, tracking shelf-life items, and managing inventory across 100+ acres of laydown yards. I also used Bechtel's procurement systems to monitor priority receipts and built an Excel KPI dashboard analyzing prior-year receipt data to surface trends and flag potential bottlenecks.

The experience ended with presenting my time there and a priorities project to about 100 people, helping supply chain leadership make more informed decisions. It was a front-row seat to what government contracting, active construction zones, and serious compliance requirements actually look like in practice.
